SiteRadarBot

SiteRadarBot is the bot of SiteRadar, FacTech's site analysis tool. It appears in your server logs as:

SiteRadarBot/1.0 (+https://factech.app/bot/)

What does it do?

  • It reads a site's public pages to check their technical state: page titles, links that don't open, the sitemap and similar.
  • It runs occasionally, one analysis at a time; it is not a search engine crawler that visits continuously.
  • It is slow on purpose: at most 2 requests at the same time and at least 1 second between requests. When a site gets slower it slows down further, and when the site asks it to wait (429 / 503) it waits.
  • It does not log in, fill in forms or run JavaScript. It does not store whole pages, only technical details such as titles.

It follows robots.txt

Our bot follows the SiteRadarBot rules in your robots.txt, or the User-agent: * rules when that name is not listed. It also respects Crawl-delay. It does not open pages you have disallowed.

How to block it

To keep our bot away from your whole site, add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: SiteRadarBot
Disallow: /

To close only part of the site, use a path such as Disallow: /section/.
